Dated room maintenance

Record an outage, understand its impact, and check recorded use for a date.

Implementation approval pending

The proposed contract

Keep each outage with dates, weekday rules, exact hours and a reason. Staff can record a real outage even when bookings exist, after reviewing a fresh impact list.

One overlapping date blocks a recurring placement. A weekly class cannot pass the placement or publication check while any occurrence clashes. Single-date class changes need a later feature.

Published routines stay frozen. Internal warnings identify affected publications; staff must review and publish a replacement separately.

01 · Room detail

Maintenance & history

02 · Add or correct

Review the impact

A dated window, explicit confirmation, preserved input and stale/pending feedback.

03 · Occupancy

Check a date

Bookings, maintenance, usable time and potential gaps, with A4 portrait print.

Six decisions to approve

D-1 Existing authority, correction history and permanent room retention.

D-2 Finite inclusive Dhaka dates; all days or selected weekdays; same-day hours.

D-3 Save an outage after a fresh all-term impact review, without changing bookings.

D-4 One conflicting occurrence blocks the entire recurring placement.

D-5 Fresh checks on every writer and publication; uncertain overlapping OD use blocks publication.

D-6 Preserve weekly occupancy; add an explicit date basis and matching internal print.

These are proposed operational choices. No institutional maintenance calendar or approver is assumed. Read the full boundaries and acceptance criteria.
