# OD-01B — Effective dates, carry-forward and formal teaching groups

## Inspected baseline

- OD-01A already gives coordinators a guided, revision-checked register, an impact preview and audited lifecycle actions. Pending and Verified commitments both participate in draft planning. Only Verified, active commitments enter new publications. Workload comes from explicit allocations. [OD-01A verification](../OD-01A/verification.md) · [Commitment actions](../../../src/lib/commitment-actions.ts) · [Snapshot builder](../../../src/lib/serialize.ts)
- A commitment has a weekly slot but no occurrence dates. Formal meetings already have optional effective dates, and room maintenance has a civil-date and weekday overlap rule. The scheduler, availability picker and preview also need the new bounds to agree with the conflict list. The institutional source's OD cells describe room use, so room-only commitments must still work on their own. The OD register currently works in the active term under `manage_external_commitments`. New snapshots must freeze the filtered OD row and its date labels. The historical archive validator explicitly projects permitted fields. ## Proposed decisions

### D-1 — Effective dates

- Structured commitments get an optional **Effective dates** pair. Both blank mean **Whole term**. Otherwise enter both inclusive Asia/Dhaka civil dates, in order, within the administrative term, with at least one occurrence of the chosen weekday. A one-day commitment uses matching dates on its chosen weekday. An unresolved note has no effective dates. Existing rows keep whole-term behavior.
- Keep the known-resource rules from OD-01A. A dated OD clash needs the same weekday, overlapping minutes and at least one occurrence of that weekday in the intersection of the two effective ranges. Use the formal meeting's existing dates when available and term bounds for a new whole-term meeting. Legacy unknown dates keep conservative checks. Weekly planning uses administrative dates and does not infer that another department follows the CSE holiday or teaching calendar.
- Use the same occurrence rule in the OD impact preview, routine conflicts, placement and availability checks, and auto-scheduling. Apply the OD bounds to the maintenance preview/save/publication checks and room deactivation. A past range ceases blocking room deactivation under the existing future-use rule. Keep historical room and teacher references for deletion checks. Date edits send a Verified record back to Pending and appear in history.
- Show the range with the weekly slot in the register and planning views, including teacher views. In weekly routine screen and print, show a dated standalone booking once in its normal OD cell with the range attached to its label. The formal-meeting CSV retains its current layout and scope.

### D-2 — Explicit carry-forward during term preparation

- Offer an optional **Cross-department commitments** section in Terms → Prepare. Start with none selected. The existing source, destination, one-time receipt and activation rules apply. Reading and copying commitment details requires `manage_external_commitments` in addition to the current term and cohort preparation permissions. Staff with only the existing preparation permissions can still prepare cohorts/grid. Keep detailed commitment information out of the general receipt view for users without the commitments permission.
- Offer active, valid standalone records, including unresolved notes. Exclude archived records, linked records and legacy rows that require correction, with a short reason. A whole-term record proposes whole term in the destination. A bounded record requires a new explicit destination date pair. Keep the weekly slot, teacher, room, course, audience and credits as applicable, and retain unresolved note text. The original dates and supporting notes remain in the source record. Refuse selections with inactive resources or new room-maintenance overlaps, with the relevant item and reason.
- The review shows selected source facts, destination dates, resources and the Pending status. Commit rechecks the selection, source revisions, term and room evidence. A changed decision or concurrent write requires a fresh review. Copies, existing preparation writes, receipt and audits commit together. An issued receipt is read-only, including a receipt from before this change. Let selected commitments count as preparation when there are no cohort/grid changes. Leave the source records untouched and record each source-to-destination commitment ID in the receipt and destination audit history.

### D-3 — Reconfirmation after activation

- A copy starts active in its destination and **Pending**, with an internal reference to its source term and commitment. Verification evidence is collected afresh. The copy has no workload allocation, payment data or teaching-group link. The draft receipt displays it as awaiting review. Staff manage it from the commitments register after activation.
- Pending copies follow ordinary draft planning rules when the destination is active. They remain tentative teacher/room blockers and stay out of published OD rows. The destination's authorized commitment manager reconfirms with the existing **Verify** action and a new confirmation note after checking the booking with the counterpart department. On carried records, Verify rechecks live resource eligibility and maintenance. Pending has no automatic expiry or verification. A record that does not continue can be archived; a dated record's range limits its planning effects even while it remains pending.

### D-4 — Link an existing formal teaching group

- An active structured teaching or combined commitment can be linked to an existing group in the same active term. The commitment manager explicitly confirms it is the same class and records a reason. Candidate groups must have an active fixed meeting in the routine, without pending reconciliation, with the same weekday and start/end time, the assigned teacher and, when the commitment reserves a room, the assigned physical room. One matching meeting must cover every occurrence in the commitment's effective range. Filter candidates by these facts and show course, audience, teacher, room, time and dates for review. This action creates no group, meeting, enrollment or allocation. Room-only commitments and unresolved notes remain standalone.
- If an allocation already counts the commitment, refuse the link and explain the workload dependency. A valid link makes the formal meeting the only planning entry. Keep the commitment, verification state and full history visible in the register. Omit its duplicate OD entry from conflicts, availability, maintenance impact, routine/teacher output and new snapshots. Validity is independent of verification. The formal meeting still participates in all existing checks and output.
- Recheck coverage against current formal meeting facts whenever a link is used to suppress an OD booking. If the meeting is moved, cancelled, removed or loses coverage, label the link **Needs review**, restore the standalone planning effect immediately, and block publication until coverage is restored or the link is removed. Do not block routine edits just to preserve a match. Hold linked group hard deletion until its links are explicitly removed. **Remove link** asks for a reason and shows the restored planning effect when active. Archived links can be removed without reactivating the record. Keep current permissions, active-term context and revision guards on all OD actions. History shows the group and reason for link/removal.

### D-5 — Preserve history and freeze the new projection

- Propose forward-only migration `0023` for optional effective dates, a same-term teaching-group reference and carry provenance. Pre-migration commitments, audits and snapshots retain their values and meaning. Provenance must survive removal of a mistaken source entry. Preserve existing deletion protections and workload, attendance and claims records.
- New publication snapshots freeze effective dates for the standalone Verified commitments they include. Propose snapshot version 5 with the existing time grid and allowlisted OD date fields. Keep historical versions on their existing readers and renderers. Public and internal saved routines derive only from their frozen data. Keep OD link IDs, source and carry details, confirmation notes and audit records internal. A concurrent OD or meeting edit must not result in publishing a stale suppression decision or stale range.

## Owner review

Approve or amend D-1–D-5. In particular, D-3 proposes that an existing authorized commitment manager reconfirms with the counterpart department after activation, and that Pending has no automatic deadline while date bounds still apply. D-4 proposes matching an existing scheduled group and restoring the original booking if coverage drifts. New formal groups still use the existing course, offering and group workflow.
