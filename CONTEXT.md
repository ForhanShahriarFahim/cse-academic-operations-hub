# Academic Operations Language

This glossary names the distinct academic facts managed by the portal. It is intentionally independent of screens, tables, and implementation choices.

## Calendar and audience

**Academic term**:
One Spring or Summer operating period in an academic year.
_Avoid_: Session, semester

**Cohort**:
A stable group of students identified by programme, HSC or Diploma stream, and batch label across academic terms.
_Avoid_: Semester, term batch

**Cohort placement**:
A cohort's curriculum semester and verified size for one academic term.
_Avoid_: Batch identity

## Teaching and scheduling

**Catalog course**:
The enduring definition of a course, including its academic credits and type.
_Avoid_: Class, term course

**Course offering**:
A catalog course offered to a particular cohort in one academic term.
_Avoid_: Catalog course

**Teaching group**:
The delivery audience for one or more course offerings; shared or merged delivery belongs to one group without merging the cohorts themselves.
_Avoid_: Batch, course

**Meeting**:
One recurring scheduled class event with a day, exact time, teacher assignment, and room assignment.
_Avoid_: Attendance session, course

**External commitment**:
A cross-department teaching or room obligation whose known facts constrain planning without inventing unknown details.
_Avoid_: OD class

**Published routine**:
An approved, immutable version of the timetable made available to the public from its own snapshot.
_Avoid_: Working draft

## Attendance and workload

**Student**:
A person whose identity is independent of a home department or any particular course enrollment.
_Avoid_: Roster row

**Course enrollment**:
A student's participation in a teaching group for one academic term, including local, external, or merged audience context.
_Avoid_: Student identity

**Attendance session**:
One dated occurrence of a teaching group for which student attendance is recorded.
_Avoid_: Academic term, meeting

**Catalog credit**:
The academic credit assigned to a course; a sessional course may carry one catalog credit.
_Avoid_: Workload credit-hour

**Workload credit-hour**:
The term-policy measure of a teacher's assigned teaching load; a one-credit sessional course currently counts as two workload credit-hours.
_Avoid_: Catalog credit

**Extra-load class**:
One dated class claimed by an eligible teacher above the term's workload threshold, with payment calculated from the approved per-class rate.
_Avoid_: Workload allocation

## People and governance

**Teacher record**:
The academic identity used for teaching assignments, workload, routines, and historical reports, whether or not the teacher can sign in.
_Avoid_: Portal user

**Portal user**:
An invited person permitted to sign in and receive one or more role assignments; the user may optionally link to a teacher record.
_Avoid_: Teacher record

**Institutional decision**:
A reviewed choice of policy or source interpretation with an owner, evidence, effective term, and affected operations.
_Avoid_: Unverified default
